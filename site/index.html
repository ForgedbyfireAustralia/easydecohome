Design help

Measure before you order

  1. Wall width. Measure at bench height. Leave 50 mm each side if it sits between walls.
  2. Waste and water. Note their heights. On a wall-hung unit they need to sit inside the cabinet.
  3. Wall backing. Wall-hung vanities fix to studs or noggins. Check with your installer.

Questions

Do you install?

No. We supply the vanity and your plumber or carpenter fits it. That's how we keep the price down.

How does delivery work?

Your order ships direct from our factory to your door. Freight is worked out at checkout from the country you're shipping to, and taps ordered with a vanity travel in the same carton.

Are taps included?

CONFIRM Tapware sold in Australia needs WaterMark certification. Only list taps the factory can certify.
